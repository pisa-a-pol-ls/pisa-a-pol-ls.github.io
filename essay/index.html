English Expository Essay Reading / Writing

English expository essay

An expository essay is a type of academic writing that explains, clarifies, or informs the reader about a topic. It is objective, meaning you avoid personal opinions or emotional bias. It is highly useful for walking a reader through a complex process or concept.

Thesis statement

A thesis statement is the central idea of your essay — the one sentence that tells the reader what your essay will explain. It is usually placed at the end of the introduction. It acts as a roadmap, telling the reader what to expect and outlining the points you will prove.

Cohesive writing

By cohesive, it means how well your sentences and paragraphs stick together on the surface level. It acts as the linguistic "glue" that prevents your text from feeling disjointed, making it effortless for the reader to follow your thoughts.

Coherent writing

Coherent writing refers to the logic and organization of your ideas. The logical connection and smooth flow of ideas across a text ensures the writing makes sense as a whole. Sentences and paragraphs build upon each other rather than jumping randomly.

Takeaway

It's the main idea or insight you want the reader to remember after reading your essay. It is usually placed at the end of the essay to help readers summarize and reflect on the whole content.
