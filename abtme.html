About me

Hello! I’m William. My Chinese name is pronounced the same way as the English one (威廉）. I was given the English name first, so my dad decided to transliterate the English one to Mandarin. I was born in Manila, the capital of Philippine, and I am biracial of Taiwanese and Filipino. Unfortunately, I was deprived of the opportunity to use Tagalog ever since I moved to Taiwan at the age of five, so I can barely use my actual mother tongue to communicate with my relatives in Philippine.

I’m currently in the department of Foreign Languages and Literature, and I mostly take linguistic courses like Psycholinguistic, pragmatic, SLA and Phonology. I plan on taking Intermediate Spanish and beginning Japanese in next semester. If possible, I would also try to learn Tagalog on my own with assistance from my mom. If you are also interested in Spanish, feel free to chat with me so that we can both improve our Spanish speaking.

As for my personality, I tend to be more individualistic. I like hanging out with my friends, but I still prefer doing stuff on my own. I rarely call my friends out to travel or play games. I don’t think I’m that introverted but I just like enjoying my me time.
